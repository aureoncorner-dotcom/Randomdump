The strongest supported components include a White House–DOJ religious-policy structure; a dated sequence linking Todd Blanche's disclosed crypto interests, ethics obligations, a department-wide enforcement memorandum, and later reported transactions; an enumerated Palantir contract footprint and a narrow CMS/HHS → ICE → Palantir data-custody route; public media amplification and bounded private-access structures; and a durable judicial selection, confirmation, and advocacy route. `[Advanced components: RP-01–RP-05; CR-01–CR-05; TC-02, TC-03; PL-01–PL-03, PL-06; PA-01, PA-03, PA-05, PA-06; JU-01–JU-04, JU-06–JU-08, JU-11. The distinction between a route and a consequence is the paper's central discipline. Institutional power often travels through ordinary mechanisms—appointments, executive orders, guidance, contracting, data-sharing, media pressure, professional networks, nominations, scheduling, and disclosure rules. Demonstrating such a route is analytically important. It is not, without the missing join, proof of unlawful capture, corrupt intent, or common command.

## I. How to read the record


### 1. Authority and source types


Primary instruments control the factual component they actually prove. Independently verified reporting may carry a component when the underlying instrument is unavailable, but it does not become primary merely because it quotes or links one. The controlling reconciliation governs later corrections; the canonical handoff supplies the prior claim-state and continuing retired-arrow, null-result, counterevidence, and do-not-state boundaries. The raw investigation transcript and its PDF or DOCX exports are provenance-recovery material, not authority over later correction. Model repetition and agreement supply no corroboration. `[Revision-control basis: controlling register §§1, 8–9. Internal provenance status: LOG-01, pending as described in Appendix A.]`


`SRC-P` identifies primary provenance for a document or statement; it does not make every proposition inside the document true. A party motion is primary evidence that the party filed or quoted a representation. An agency report is primary evidence of the agency's position. An issuer announcement is primary evidence of the announcement. Only a court's own order is described as a court finding, and only at the procedural stage the order reached.


The paper distinguishes the following source roles whenever the distinction matters:


| Label | Meaning in this paper |
|---|---|
| **Court finding/order** | What a court actually found or ordered at the stated procedural stage; a preliminary order is not a final merits judgment. |
| **Party representation or argument** | What a litigant asserted, quoted, or requested in a filing; it is not converted into a court finding. |
| **Official position** | What an agency, office, official, or transaction party said; the existence of the position may be established even when its underlying reasoning is unavailable. |
| **Verified reporting** | Independently reported material, classed `P3 / SRC-V`; its source-family dependence remains visible. |
| **Leak-derived material** | Material reported from an authenticated or verified leak, classed `P4 / SRC-L`; it proves no more than the verified artifact and reporting support. |
| **Interested-party allegation or self-report** | A complaint, oversight allegation, advocate's claim, or organization's account of its own activity; it establishes the allegation or announcement, not its truth in full. |
| **Inference or bounded search status** | An analytic conclusion or statement about the reviewed record, classed `X`; it is not an external-world fact or proof of universal absence. |


### 2. Findings have three dimensions


Each proposition has a finding type—fact, architecture, legal element, causal join, or intent/consideration—separate from its evidence state and investigation action. Thus an architecture can be established while the decisive causal join remains open. `NOT ESTABLISHED` means the reviewed record does not carry the proposition; it is not automatically a finding that the proposition is false. `RETIRED/CORRECTED` remains visible so a defeated arrow cannot return through later prose.


`HOLD` means preserve the row but do not publish it as a finding until the named instrument or test is satisfied. `CLOSE ON CURRENT RECORD` means the existing record does not justify further affirmative treatment, while allowing reopening on materially different evidence. A `join` is a source-bearing link between components; a recurring name, shared vendor, adjacent date, or common setting is not a join by itself.


### 3. Source concentration and the non-random sample


Sources are counted by evidence family, not headline count. Reuters and AP applied different classifications to one OGE filing; the Medicaid custody record is concentrated in one federal docket family; much of the 2026 Dialog coverage traces to one leaked dataset; several judicial-benefit rows depend heavily on ProPublica reporting and linked records; and JCN's spending figures are interested self-reports rather than audited totals. Repetition within those families does not multiply independent support. `[Sources/families: SRC-TC-001–SRC-TC-003; SRC-PL-008–SRC-PL-012; SRC-PA-004, SRC-PA-005, SRC-PA-007; SRC-JU-005–SRC-JU-014. Controlling concentration ledger: register §7.]`


The actor set is non-random. Symbolic material influenced historical selection but supplies zero evidentiary weight in this paper. No disposition is upgraded by symbol, proximity, chronology alone, or model consensus. The paper therefore makes no prevalence, exceptionalism, or “unusualness” claim without a valid comparison class.


### 4. Archival-preservation gap


At the cutoff, public sources were preserved as live locators and retrieval dates only. No project-held evidentiary snapshot or cryptographic hash was created. A live URL—even when it points to a PDF or third-party mirror—is a retrieval path, not a project archive; later content changes or link failure cannot be reconstructed from this pass. Capture and hashing remain pending. That preservation work would improve auditability but would not itself change a claim disposition.


### 5. Null results and LOG-01


A failed search is not proof that an instrument does not exist. An unanswered request is not proof that no clearance exists. A future no-record response would establish only the responding office's scoped search result. Selected rows that yielded no support are therefore **null results**, not negative controls or exonerations. The current workspace does not contain a standalone, fully reproducible `LOG-01` with complete query strings, databases, filters, timestamps, hit lists, exclusion decisions, and hashes. `LOG-01` is consequently marked **PENDING REPRODUCIBILITY** in Appendix A and cannot carry an external-world absence claim.


## II. Religious-policy routing: a public route and an open consequence


Executive Order 14205 placed the White House Faith Office in the Domestic Policy Council and authorized outside consultation, policy recommendations, agency coordination, federal-grant access work, private-sector consultation, agency liaisons, and collaboration with the Attorney General on religious-liberty enforcement concerns. Those are primary-instrument facts. They establish an institutional route, not improper preference. `[Claims: RP-01–RP-03. Source: SRC-RP-001, P1/SRC-P executive order.]`


Executive Order 14202 separately created a DOJ-centered Task Force to Eradicate Anti-Christian Bias, chaired by the Attorney General and composed of officials from a broad set of departments and offices. DOJ's official account identifies senior participants at the inaugural meeting. The order establishes the public machinery; DOJ's account establishes the agency's official participation record. Neither establishes an off-record hierarchy or independently validates every participant statement. `[Claim: RP-04. Sources: SRC-RP-002, P1/SRC-P executive order; SRC-RP-003, P2/SRC-P official agency account.]`


Executive Order 14291 created a Religious Liberty Commission with an advisory relationship to the Faith Office and Domestic Policy Council and with DOJ support. The same primary instrument terminates the Commission on 4 July 2026 unless extended. No extension instrument was recovered, so the paper states the Commission's creation and authorized route but holds any present-tense claim that it remained operational after that date. `[Claim: RP-05. Source: SRC-RP-004, P1/SRC-P executive order. Missing instrument: continuation or extension record.]`


The route crossed from organization into administration. DOJ's July 2026 official announcement says updated religious-liberty guidance reached federal programs, employment, contracting, rulemaking, and enforcement. A 2026 task-force report is an official government position and evidence of a multi-agency output; its contested assertions about prior conduct do not become independently established merely because DOJ published them. `[Claim: RP-05. Sources: SRC-RP-005 and SRC-RP-006, P2/SRC-P official agency materials.]`


The corridor's supportable finding is therefore structural: religious-policy interpretation had a formal route from the White House through DOJ and into named administrative domains. Formal coordination around religious liberty is also a conventional executive function and the strongest ordinary explanation on the present record. `RP-06` remains open because no source-bearing chain identifies a particular outside organization, a pre-decision request, a resulting grant, contract, enforcement, or rulemaking action, the governing exception, and a relevant comparator. `[Claim: RP-06, CAUSAL JOIN / OPEN / HOLD. No supporting source. Missing instrument: REQ-RP-01.]`
