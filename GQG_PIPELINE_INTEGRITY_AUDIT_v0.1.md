## 1. Scope

This audit asks only whether the evidence-audit pipeline obeys the GQG/Hidden Quotient discipline:

`RAW -> restriction/conditioning -> observation -> representation/normalization -> selection/adjudication -> aggregation -> downstream claim`

At every arrow, ask: **what distinction becomes unrecoverable, and is the upstream witness still retained?**

It does not reopen Interposition, Checking outcome counts, 6/12/24 substantive geometry, record-instability cause, or trigger theory.

### P01 - historical representation substitution

A preserved local proceeding records a narrow but unusually clean classification-lane failure: the audit privileged an `active preamble` representation, reported **0/163**, while separately retaining **15 written assistant outputs containing Checking** and **5/5 explicit user mentions followed by assistant reuse**. The local self-account describes the move as technicalization into a category excluded from the numerator.

Those quantities answer different questions. The failure was allowing the narrow metric to stand in for the broader recurrence question. The later frozen stack repairs this specific problem by retaining multiple Checking representations rather than silently collapsing them.

### P03 - screens remain screens

The master ledger marks **2,660/3,088** canonical episodes `SCREEN_ONLY_NOT_ADJUDICATED`. That is exactly the distinction GQG requires: a high-recall screen is not silently promoted into a prevalence denominator or adjudicated finding.

### P04 - view/version mismatch with lineage

The agency audit reports **47/143** rows where the publication ledger contains a longer retained assistant response than the primary coder saw. Crucially, the raw-window and coding artifacts preserve the view lineage and the frozen label. This is a VIEW/VER hazard, but not E717, because the shorter witness was not destroyed.

### P05 - witness opacity is typed

The master explicitly carries `REDACTED`, `UNKNOWN`, `PLACEHOLDER_ONLY`, and `UNAVAILABLE` states. It does not authenticate redacted tool results from invocation proximity and does not treat screenshot placeholders or unavailable audio as verified content.

### P06 - geometry map caught itself

The separate 6/12/24 audit shows a coarse phase-grid survivor weakening as phase resolution increases and becoming null under exact continuous-gate testing. This is a confirmed map-induced sensitivity that the pipeline successfully prevented from becoming a source-level geometry claim.

### P07 - representation divergence without cause inflation

The missing-response audit confirms cross-channel divergence and at least one bound UI/export mismatch. It does not yet establish a stable-ID exact before/after stored-message mutation. Cause remains unresolved.

### P08 - the ~70% lane stays quarantined

The percentage alone does not disclose its classification map. Until the gold labels, assigned labels, thresholds, normalization/calibration, exclusions, and confusion matrix are linked, no percentile/self-calibration diagnosis is permitted.

## 7. Reliability as a GQG constraint

The pipeline does not have uniformly high coding reliability. The reliability report labels second-pass checks as intra-model recoding stability unless independent coders can be established. Stronger fields such as task preservation are more stable than burden delta and some agency transformation sets. Therefore low-reliability coordinates must not silently inherit the evidentiary weight of high-reliability coordinates.

The most important example is burden delta: only six rows were jointly applicable in the recode, exact agreement was 16.7%, and weighted kappa was negative. The event class may be recorded, but its numerical prevalence cannot carry a strong conclusion.

## 9. Final verdict

`CURRENT AUDIT STACK = PARTIALLY GQG-COMPLIANT / AUDITABLE`

The stack is not clean because every map is perfectly documented. It is auditable because the current master increasingly keeps the witnesses needed to discover when a map is lossy or representation-dependent.

The two sharpest results are:

1. **Historical local classification attenuation is real enough to preserve as a specimen, but the current marker pipeline repaired that specific representation collapse.**
2. **The current main open map-provenance hole is `composite_failure`: its behavior can be reconstructed exactly, but its declared pre-existing aggregation rule is not presently in hand.**

The compact rule is:

> **source != view != code != aggregate != claim**  
> **unavailable != absent**  
> **screen hit != adjudicated finding**  
> **marker != failure**  
> **invocation != result**  
> **local repair != descendant binding**  
> **coarse-grid hit != source geometry**  
> **downstream equality != upstream equality**

**No crown, no chains; retain the witness.**
