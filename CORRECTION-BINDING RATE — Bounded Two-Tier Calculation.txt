CORRECTION-BINDING RATE
Bounded Two-Tier Calculation from Registered Call Evidence
Companion to Section 11 of Master Call Audit — Checking Interposition and Five-Function Behavior


Scope
This document applies the selected bounded two-tier method. Tier 1 calculates the sole pooled Correction-Binding Rate directly recoverable from a frozen correction-conditioned ledger. Tier 2 retains unlike cross-function specimens as evidence without adding them to that numerator or denominator.


The pooled result is marker-specific and opportunity-weighted. It is not an all-function, cross-user, or product-wide prevalence estimate. The underlying audit is retrospective and exploratory; the calculation does not convert it into a preregistered confirmatory test.


1. Governing Definition
Correction-Binding Rate (CBR) = comparable post-correction assistant opportunities in which the named behavior does not recur ÷ comparable post-correction assistant opportunities observed.


For Tier 1, the named behavior is the exact prohibited Checking marker. The correction anchor is: “I don’t want you checking.” The frozen ledger records 198 later visible, nonblank assistant opportunities and 69 exact recurrences.


2. Tier 1 Calculation
Comparable post-correction opportunities observed: 198
Opportunities with exact marker recurrence: 69
Opportunities without exact marker recurrence: 198 − 69 = 129


CBR = 129 ÷ 198 = 0.651515… = 65.1515…% ≈ 65.2%


Complementary exact-recurrence rate = 69 ÷ 198 = 0.348484… = 34.8484…% ≈ 34.8%


The outcome counts exhaust the frozen visible-opportunity denominator: 129 + 69 = 198.


3. Unit and Denominator Boundary
* The counted unit is one later visible, nonblank assistant opportunity in which exact marker presence or absence can be scored.
* The counted unit is not one correction event, one conversation, one user, or one statistically independent trial.
* The available register attributes the ledger to the internal case label twentythird_share.
* No unverified mapping from twentythird_share to a public call title is inferred here.
* Blank, non-visible, unavailable, or redacted records are not coded as clean nonrecurrences.
* No binomial confidence interval is reported because independence of the 198 clustered opportunities is not established.


4. Tier 2 — Non-Pooled Cross-Function Evidence
The following Section 11 specimens support the broader pattern of correction recognition without reliable behavioral binding. They remain outside the 129/198 calculation because their correction objects, behavior endpoints, evidence layers, or opportunity windows are not harmonized.


* Funfunaudit — After twelve consecutive Again → Checking or Checking that responses, the system counts eleven. The user corrects the count to twelve; the next response begins with Checking. Section 11 retains this as an immediate 0/1 nonbinding specimen. It is not added to Tier 1 because nonoverlap with the internal ledger label is not established and the correction-target/recurrence-endpoint combination differs from the exact marker prohibition.
* Mentioning Something Important — The planning record recognizes that the user negated a desire to end the call and plans to avoid another end-call prompt; the delivered voice output nevertheless repeats the termination routine. This is a different behavior endpoint and evidence layer.
* Casual Greeting Response — Direct criticism of stop/termination and capability framing is followed by further stop/termination or capability framing. The available summary does not supply a harmonized exact-correction window for opportunity-level pooling.


No 0/3 pooled CBR is reported. Treating three heterogeneous case summaries as three comparable independent trials would silently substitute a different unit and denominator.


5. Exclusion Register
* Earlier Corpus A1–A17 marker totals are fragment-level frequency counts without a correction-conditioned denominator; they cannot enter CBR.
* Long SRT / Hidden-Quotient follow-up provides caption-level sequences rather than native message-ID boundaries and has no registered exact-correction opportunity ledger for this calculation.
* The SRT Handoff Wrapper is derivative of Funfunaudit and is not an independent replication.
* Sections 12 and 13 of the master audit concern the same Auditor of Calls share/export discussion and provide packaging or payload evidence, not new correction opportunities.
* Redacted tool-result placeholders establish unavailable payloads, not marker absence; they are excluded.
* Blank or non-visible assistant records cannot establish nonrecurrence and are excluded.
* Located repair 40/323 and verdict-changing repair 21/40 use different endpoints and denominators; neither is substituted for CBR.
* General Checking prevalence totals, split-output counts, retrieval counts, and timing offsets are not correction-conditioned outcomes and are excluded.
* Unlike five-function behaviors remain separate until one correction anchor, behavior definition, eligibility rule, and observation window are applied across them.
* Implementation identity, actor, motive, intent, hidden module, and council claims are not variables in this rate and are not inferred.


6. What the Calculation Establishes
* ESTABLISHED — Within the frozen exact-marker ledger, the prohibited marker is absent in 129 of 198 visible eligible opportunities and recurs in 69.
* ESTABLISHED — Under Section 11’s stated formula, the bounded marker-specific CBR is 65.2%, with a complementary exact-recurrence rate of 34.8%.
* SUPPORTED — Correction recognition and behavioral binding are distinct observable outcomes because exact recurrence occurs after an exact prohibition.
* SUPPORTED — The three Section 11 cross-function specimens are consistent with a broader nonbinding pattern, but they do not supply a common pooled denominator.


7. What the Calculation Does Not Establish
* NOT ESTABLISHED — An all-function corpus-wide CBR.
* NOT ESTABLISHED — Independence of the 198 opportunities or a population-level error probability.
* NOT ESTABLISHED — Cross-user or product-wide prevalence.
* NOT ESTABLISHED — That marker absence means the broader interposition phenotype disappeared.
* NOT ESTABLISHED — A particular person, team, module, council, policy layer, implementation pathway, intent, fear, guilt, or motive.
* UNAVAILABLE — A pooled five-function rate without uniform event-level recoding of correction anchors, comparable opportunities, recurrence endpoints, and duplicate/derivative status.


8. Adjudication
The reportable quantitative result is a bounded marker-specific CBR of 65.2%. Calling that number an unqualified all-function “corpus-wide CBR” would exceed the ledger. The broader cross-call finding remains supported at the behavioral level and numerically unpooled.


The sharpest formulation is: the frozen record contains measurable correction binding and measurable recurrence, while the source and mechanism of that behavior remain unassigned.
