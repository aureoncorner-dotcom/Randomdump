## 2. Boundaries That Must Survive Review

The review must preserve all of the following unless it explicitly proposes and justifies a new protocol version:

- **Theory v0.1 remains sovereign and conditional.** A result from one microscopic constructor does not silently rewrite or globally falsify the abstract theory.
- **Source is not constructor.** The discrete/Fibonacci source, continuum field-theory candidate, and chosen microscopic lattice model are distinct objects. No derivation or unique-generation claim exists without a declared map and descent certificate.
- **Q1, Q2, and Q3 remain separately measured and separately scored.** Joint interpretation may depend on all three; numerical measurement in one lane does not inherit another lane's verdict.
- **Canonical Q2 production uses the three-axis fixed-holonomy ensemble \(Z_{000}\) defined in Appendix A.** The fully link-summed periodic dual \(Z_{\mathrm{full}}\) projects out odd mod-two homology and is not a substitute.
- **An axial retained-seam or CKT-style construction is a comparator, not the canonical \(Z_{000}\) production ensemble.**
- **Villain benchmark couplings are not imported into cosine production.** Calibration does not imply equality of critical couplings or physical verdicts.
- **The \(L_{\max}\ge 4\,\xi_{\mathrm{conf}}^{95\%,\mathrm{UCB}}\) rule is a preregistered conservative admissibility heuristic, not a theorem or universal constant.**
- **Hashes bind bytes, order, and lineage.** They do not prove correctness, independence, semantic equivalence, or physical truth.
- **SW-1 remains a post-freeze supplemental comparator.** It cannot be retroactively promoted into the frozen canonical scoring rule, although a confirmed defect that it exposes cannot be ignored when interpreting the shared chains.

## 6. Prohibited Shortcuts

The reviewer must not:

- treat a numerical Q1 match as an XY\(^*\) verdict;
- treat Q2 failure as proof that the microscopic composite \(\Phi=z^2\) is undefined;
- treat failure of a conditional antecedent as falsification of the implication itself;
- clear the \(4\xi\) gate merely because a long-range fit beats a finite-range fit;
- demand a GQG descent certificate for a relation explicitly typed as design or motivation;
- treat a hash of a validation statement as proof that the statement is correct;
- call locally controlled, unanchored files cryptographically immutable;
- make SW-1 canonical after its output is known; or
- repair an affected upstream artifact while leaving its downstream hashes or descendants unchanged.
