Thermodynamic Coordination Model
Stripped architecture — CC0
Core Variables
E_in: Energy input from available sources
W_out: Work performed by the system
S_gen: Entropy generation (waste, friction, loss)
R_cap: Regenerative capacity of the local environment
Net Energy Balance
NEB = E_in - (W_out + S_gen)
Regenerative Coupling
R_cap(t+1) = R_cap(t) + η · (resource_circulation · S_gen)
Waste outputs, when efficiently coupled back into the system, increase the environment's capacity to regenerate. η is the coupling efficiency — how much of the waste actually gets redirected rather than lost.
Phase Regions (descriptive, not sequential)
Phase I — Extraction: S_gen > E_in. System is consuming its substrate faster than it recovers.
Phase II — Maintenance: NEB ≈ 0. Stable but no surplus. All energy goes to staying operational.
Phase III — Resonance: NEB > 0. Surplus exists. Waste begins feeding regeneration.
Phase IV — Garden: R_cap increases each cycle. The system's presence actively improves its substrate.
Stability Condition
Surplus becomes the default output.
Connection to TOH
Phase I = membrane bypass. Phase II = membrane pinch. Phase III = stable O→R→S→R→O loop. Phase IV = substrate regenerating under sustained loop stability.
