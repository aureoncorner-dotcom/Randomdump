## Boundary

This ledger contains checkable events, dates, measurements, arithmetic, source status, and research tasks. It does not treat symbolic resonance as evidence. Symbolic readings live in the companion *Timekeeper Symbolic Atlas v1*.

The timekeeper question is de-agented:

> Whose interests were served by this timing—and is there independent evidence that anyone chose it, rather than several clocks merely converging?

Convergence is the null model. A timing cluster is a prompt for inspection, never a finding on its own.

### Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Hard evidence | Directly supported by a strong primary or official source |
| Reported | Publicly reported, but not independently established as settled fact |
| Supported interpretation | A bounded inference from documented facts |
| Research task | Requires a stronger source, calculation, or chain of custody |
| Retired/corrected | Earlier wording was false, overstated, or lane-confused |
| Not load-bearing | May orient inquiry but may not support a causal conclusion |
