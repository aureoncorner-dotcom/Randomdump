## 1. Symbolic/function map — hypothesis generator only

| Function | Question it may nominate | Non-evidentiary counter-constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Crown | Who visibly controls a decision? | Exitable, bounded authority |
| Architect | Who designs the option set or dependency? | Forkability; no compulsory dependency |
| Mediator | Who converts a constituency or frame into authorized interpretation? | Wisdom without compulsory doctrine |
| Mouth / amplifier | Who transmits or scales the frame? | Message carriage without ownership of meaning |
| Signer | Who converts a proposal into formal authority? | Proportion, review, and correction |
| Gatekeeper | Who controls access, permission, or exclusion? | A usable bypass or refusal path |
| Enforcer | Who executes classification, investigation, or coercion? | Boundary defense rather than compulsory jurisdiction |
| Scribe | Who controls records, provenance, or official history? | Versioned provenance; no silent rewrite |
| Merchant / allocator | Who moves money, contracts, or access? | Transparent circulation and accountable allocation |
| Timekeeper | Was timing intentionally selected? | Convergence is the default until chosen timing is proved |
| Auditor / counter-witness | Did review independently correct the system? | Inspectable findings and effective remedy |

The symbolic map may nominate a test; it cannot appoint an occupant, prove coordination, transmit agency, establish wrongdoing, or select the evidence.

## 4. Consolidated do-not-state register

- Symbolic fit appoints an occupant or proves coordination.
- Timing symmetry transmits agency or meaning.
- Four-priesthood language identifies a literal or secret organization.
- Horseman imagery establishes an outcome before the causal spine is complete.
- Disclosed crypto interests establish laundering, rug pulls, secret transfers, or corruption.
- The WLF/Binance transaction purchased or caused Zhao’s pardon.
- Trump’s structural crypto-policy overlap is automatically a §208 crime.
- Blanche violated §208 before an authoritative adjudication.
- Patel selected or influenced Palantir, or retained an ongoing holding.
- Gabbard had a continuing personal tech/crypto conflict after the documented sale date.
- Hegseth had a personal Palantir interest or continuing defense-stock alignment after divestiture.
- Bondi favored Pfizer, TMTG, Ballard, or a former client merely because a relationship was disclosed.
- Johnson’s residence/access arrangement shaped policy, constituted a gift, or was corrupt without a pre-decisional influence join.

- The public Faith Office–DOJ route proves secrecy, criminality, illegitimate religion, or improper influence.
- An oversight body is captured merely because it is part of government or produced an unfavorable result.
