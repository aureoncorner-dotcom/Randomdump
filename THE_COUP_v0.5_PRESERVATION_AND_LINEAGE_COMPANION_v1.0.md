### B. v0.3 §9 — The Next Test Must Match the Boundary Hypothesis

> The correct 985.5-kya test is now a preregistered seam test.

> Freeze the 13.5-kyr period and present-based origin before scoring. Enumerate every grid seam in the same 400–1500-kya search interval. Blind the coders to seam identity. For every seam, measure whether independently sourced observations organize into stable before/after lobes, whether feature composition differs across the seam, and whether the result survives deletion of broad intervals, disputed chronologies, repeated specimens, and same-model-family evidence.

> Penalize wide intervals by their temporal width. Compare the target against every competing seam and against randomly offset grids. Require the 985.5 seam to rank unusually high and remain stable across reasonable window sizes. A strong result is not “some evidence is nearby.” A strong result is that the predeclared seam repeatedly maximizes an independently coded change-of-regime score.

> This test can fail. That is why it can work.

> The political instrument uses the same architecture. For each alleged capture event, identify the clock, compiler, address, reset rule, binder, carried residual, review route, bypass cost, and exit condition. The result is not a mood or a cast list. It is an auditable map of who can make time binding and whether the next cycle inherits the truth.
