Nitrogen Reservoirs in Fulgurites - Lab Handoff
Objective: resolve the reservoir before naming the nitrogen. No reservoir, no claim.  Boundary: source-bound condensation only; no added mechanism claims. R1-R4 are specimen reservoirs; B is a procedural/background competitor, not a fifth reservoir.  Definitions: R1 = lattice/structural N bonded as Si-N or Si-O-N; R2 = N-bearing molecular gas occluded in sealed vesicles; R3 = surface/weathering/handling N on rinds, cracks, or open surfaces; R4 = N in organic or carbonaceous residues; B = preparation/lab background competitor.

	Mandatory controls and stop rules
Analytical - full-procedure blanks for extraction/mounting/polishing/storage; N-free fused silica through identical preparation; preparation replicates; same-site protolith; sealed/open pairs; interior/rind and clean/mixed contrasts; crack/pore maps; curation audit for adhesives, consolidants, acid treatment, storage nitrate, animal/bird contamination, and handling; beam-induced C/N buildup checks.
Interpretive - SIMS SiN- alone is not Si-N; organic CN is not lattice N; sealed gas is not structural N; P/Fe redox is not reduced N. Crushing, heating, sputtering, and preparation require transformation controls. If B is variable/comparable to the candidate signal, or an assay separates no live reservoir explanation, stop or limit the conclusion.
Safety / segregation - institutional labs, trained staff, approved procedures, ventilation, and proper instrumentation only. No DIY lightning, storm collection, non-lab high-voltage rigs, sealed pressure vessels, unknown-gas extraction, mystery-material heating, or synthesis attempts. Biology and clean-N chemistry remain separate.
Expected outputs - R1 negative/upper bound; provenance-resolved N accounting; replication/extension of sealed-vesicle gas signals; spatial test of C-correlated reduced P/Fe phases; strictly controlled bioreceptivity pilot kept separate from clean chemistry; no general coupled C-N-Si mechanism.
