## 1. Blanche crypto chronology — H04, H05, H07, H08

**Source and date.** The compilation cites ProPublica reporting dated December 22, 2025 and a Campaign Legal Center complaint dated January 22, 2026. It attributes the following sequence to those sources: an ethics divestiture commitment after confirmation, a crypto-enforcement policy memorandum while holdings remained, and later transfers to adult relatives. Its stated holdings range is $159,000–$485,000. These details are retained as claims of the cited material, not a fresh reconstruction from disclosure forms.

**What was raised.** Whether the timing of policy participation, retained holdings and subsequent transfers complied with the applicable ethics obligations.

**Reported action and outcome.** The summary says the April 7, 2025 memorandum changed enforcement priorities and disbanded the National Cryptocurrency Enforcement Team. Replace “ending crypto enforcement” with that narrower description; a change of policy or team structure does not by itself establish cessation of all enforcement. The complaint raises allegations for investigation; filing it does not adjudicate them.

**Assessment and unknowns.** The supplied list names one news outlet and one complaint, not “two outlets plus a filed complaint.” Their factual origins may overlap and must be traced before counting independent corroboration. The legality of the transfers and any conflict is **NOT EVALUATED** here: the complete holdings and transaction records, governing ethics instruments and any relevant written determination are not assembled. A reported asset-price increase is not automatically realized personal gain. Bribery or a consideration chain remains unestablished by this packet.

## 2. CMS → ICE → Palantir custody — H19, H20, H21

**Source and date.** The compilation cites NPR reporting dated July 17, 2026; a Federal Register notice dated November 25, 2025; California DHCS material describing an agreement period; and an April 2026 CHLPI account of litigation. **Reported action and outcome.** The NPR summary describes sharing with ICE and onward sharing with Palantir. It also reports Palantir's assertion that the dataset was purged. **Assessment and unknowns.** The existence of a reported purge assertion is supported by the supplied summary. Actual deletion is **NOT EVALUATED**, not “provisionally established”: no deletion verification, copy inventory, backup scope or audit evidence is supplied. Production ingestion, subsequent enforcement use and any individual harm require their own records. The transfer claim is not erased by those open questions, and those open questions are not answered by repeating the transfer claim.

## Replacement closing assessment

The compilation contains several distinct kinds of material: documented relationships, reported actions, complaints, announcements, conditional institutional provisions and unresolved numerical aggregates. They require separate findings. The record does not support one blanket statement that the entire institutional architecture has been independently corroborated.

Retain each supported local finding. Keep a later acknowledgment separate from the response it acknowledges. Keep a deletion assertion separate from verified deletion, an announced transition separate from its execution, a scheduled sunset separate from confirmed termination, and repeated reporting separate from independent evidence origins. Further causal claims require the particular missing record; uncertainty about those claims does not erase what the record already shows.
