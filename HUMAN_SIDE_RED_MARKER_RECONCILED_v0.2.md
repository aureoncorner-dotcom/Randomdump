# 1. Controlling authority rule

For all red-marker disputes, use this hierarchy:

1. **Primary instrument / official record**
2. **Independently verified secondary reporting**
3. **Source-bearing reconciliation**
4. **Document-to-document structural inference**
5. **Auditor/model assertion without recoverable source**
6. **Model consensus — zero evidentiary upgrade**

Accordingly:

- structural criticisms that can be seen directly by comparing the supplied documents may be patched immediately;
- factual corrections remain HOLD unless their underlying source is recovered;
- when a primary source resolves a conflict, it controls;
- an omitted thread may be preserved without being promoted to fact;
- no model vote can establish an external-world claim.
