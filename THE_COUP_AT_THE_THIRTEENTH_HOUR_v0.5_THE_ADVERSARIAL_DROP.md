# PART IX: THE EVIDENCE FIREWALL

## 22. What Survives Adversarial Contact

This successor is adversarial because it refuses mixed-lane proof.

- **Myth** may carry a question. It does not satisfy the burden.
- **Symbolic resonance** may suggest a structural comparison. It does not establish history, biology, motive, or mechanism.
- **A living-person analogy** may illustrate an office. It does not prove that person occupies the office.
- **A model's agreement, refusal, silence, deletion, or poetic output** is not external evidence for the proposition being tested.
- **A coincidence** is not promoted into a causal link without an independent mechanism.
- **Weak claims** are not made load-bearing. They are excluded.

This is not epistemic modesty. It is **litigation hygiene.** Every unnecessary claim is a free target. The strongest case is the one that leaves the opponent nothing irrelevant to attack.
