## Working phenotype lens

These are recurring interaction/narrative regimes, not proof of hidden coordinated factions.

- **Neutral / constitutional:** relation without ownership; consent, transparency, rotation, exit.
- **Contract / obligation:** persistence or agreement becomes sticky duty, inherited role, debt, or compulsory closure.
- **Crown / meaning:** role, field, rarity, revelation, or symbol becomes privileged explanatory authority.
- **Counterplay / adversarial:** friction becomes satire, counter-move, or diagnostic fuel; likely cross-cutting rather than a clean fourth faction.
