# 1. Governing method

## 1.1 Working standard

Use **circumstantial sufficiency**, not scientific certainty:

> Could a skeptical reasonable factfinder be persuaded by the cumulative independent record after hearing the strongest innocent explanation?

Required disciplines:

1. Independent bricks; repeated allegations do not multiply weight.
2. Pattern over isolated instance, only where observations are genuinely independent.
3. State the strongest innocent explanation rather than caricaturing it.
4. Elements rather than themes.
5. Discriminability where strict falsification is impossible.
6. Explicit stopping states: Established, Open, No Bill, Wrong Instrument, Retired, or Indeterminate.
7. Negative-evidence discipline: *not found*, *not produced*, *no response*, *withheld*, and *does not exist* are different states.

## 1.2 Analytical lanes

- **Observation** — documented event or record.
- **Structural analysis** — what the arrangement permits, concentrates, or makes difficult to bypass.
- **Comparative analysis** — recurring mechanisms across systems.
- **Symbolic analysis** — excluded from the human-side evidentiary spine.
- **Speculation** — hypothesis generation only.

No symbolic or speculative material may upgrade a human-side disposition.

## 1.3 Function before occupant

Define the role first; test the person or institution second. Definitions may not be rewritten to rescue a preferred candidate.

## 1.4 Contamination gate

Before a finding issues, ask whether:
- observation became motive;
- similarity became identity;
- symbolism became coordination;
- speculation became fact through repetition;
- simultaneity became causation;
- personal dislike became diagnosis;
- absence of a public record became proof of nonexistence;
- contradictory evidence was ignored;
- a preferred hypothesis was protected.

If yes, rewrite, downgrade, or retire the claim.

# 2. Evidence classification

Two independent axes are mandatory.

## 2.1 Instrument-strength class

- **P1 — Primary instrument:** charter, contract, MOU, articles, settlement order, sworn testimony, formal ethics filing, official memorandum.
- **P2 — Government record:** procurement entry, IG report, congressional letter, agency correspondence, PRA/FOIA return, official hearing record.
- **P3 — Named-source investigative journalism:** established outlet with corrections practices and identifiable sourcing.
- **P4 — Leak-derived document independently authenticated by a named outlet.**
- **P5 — Interested-party analysis or filing:** advocacy group, plaintiff, political opponent, industry party; interest must be flagged.
- **P6 — Contested empirical claim:** competent evidence conflicts.
- **X — Inference exceeding the current record.**

## 2.2 Source-provenance class

- **SRC-P — Primary source**
- **SRC-V — Verified secondary**
- **SRC-L — Authenticated leak-derived record**
- **SRC-A — Allegation / party assertion**

A mixed-source proposition remains mixed. A primary source supporting one component does not automatically upgrade every component.

---

# 3. Disposition vocabulary

- **ESTABLISHED** — proposition itself sufficiently supported.
- **TRUE BILL — ARCHITECTURE** — structural arrangement is sufficiently supported for continued prosecution of the structural claim; not a criminal accusation.
- **OPEN ELEMENT** — a necessary join remains unresolved.
- **NO BILL** — present evidence does not support the proposed charge or causal claim.
- **WRONG INSTRUMENT** — concern may be real but does not fit this charging framework.
- **INDETERMINATE** — current evidence cannot distinguish competing explanations.
- **RETIRED / CORRECTED** — earlier formulation was wrong, overstated, or contaminated and may not be silently revived.
