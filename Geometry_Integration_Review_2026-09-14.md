## 3. Geometry findings applied

### Preserve the distinctions the claim requires

For a declared domain D, retained map π and witness W, equal retained records must imply equal W values for exact recovery. A valid contrary pair settles failure for that fixed universal claim. A clean partial sample does not establish universal sufficiency. The canonical pair-image repair preserves information; it does not supply a causally available measurement or choose the cheapest implementation.

Each component now includes an operational consequence. OSIIN distinguishes a design label from parameters needed for a claim. UPRR distinguishes a resource listing from suitability and release state. UBT distinguishes hours/output from upkeep and remaining capacity. Phase Lock makes the declaration common to all consequential exchanges.

Product obligations remain separate: recovery of one witness does not clear a failure in another. Target coarsening, observation refinement, source restriction and changed dynamics are explicitly different operations. An unavailable represented object is distinguished from a collision inside an attained record and from an unknown value. Metric approximation requires its own tolerance and bound; a located pair supplies a lower bound.

### Acquire context through a declared policy

Fixed batches, label-conditioned batches, and sequential acquisition are separate policy classes. The exact finite repair result assumes a complete finite nonempty domain and fixed total present coordinates with finite nonnegative costs. All attained fibers must be repairable, including any assigned probability zero. An empty separator for a known bad pair makes that menu unreachable for the claim.

The v2.6 threshold construction establishes n−1 required batch queries versus an optimal worst-case ceil(log₂ n) sequential queries, for n ≥ 2 on its unchanged finite domain. All n−1 cuts remain in the might-query inventory. The component revisions apply this distinction to observation effort versus the maintained equipment, access, skills and readiness required by the policy. Actual time, energy and benefit need their own observations. No general sequential optimizer is claimed.

Live stock and work can change during queries. The revised UPRR and interface records therefore require a declared consistent snapshot, reservation, or refresh/retry rule before treating answers as one state. Admissibility and privacy determine which observations enter the available menu.

### Let warranted findings govern the next action

The common record separates relevant evidence r, explicit finding F, current disposition C and performed action T. A faithful disposition follows the declared criterion on r. It must remain invariant when relevant evidence is unchanged and agree with the criterion; constant but wrong uncertainty is not a valid result.

A valid counterexample remains **FAILED / NOT_CLOSED** for the tested universal claim while repair may remain pending. **UNRESOLVED**, **TESTED_ONLY**, **NOT_RUN**, **INELIGIBLE**, and **UNREACHABLE** have distinct uses. A relevant contradiction, source invalidation, extraction correction, scope change or justified criterion change can reopen a finding. An unrelated unknown is not an extra prerequisite. User adjudications remain separately attributed from reviewer analysis.

Selected text A_sel, independently validated task completion A_task, acknowledgment, delivery, and applied correction are now explicitly distinguished. Acquiring an answer requires pre-answer information. The post-answer pair (O,A) already contains A. Offline presentation deletion does not establish live rerouting, reduced computation, successful continuation, or historical correction compliance. The original event retains its history.

### Restore the shared field and the upkeep account

The encounter is O ↔_ℒ S, with two endpoints, a declared shared non-sovereign field, and time/dynamics recorded separately. A translator can work within the field without becoming a compulsory authority. Membership in ℒ does not establish contact. Contact, binding consent, usable exit and absence of a compulsory third authority remain four separately assessed conditions; a positive resource or output result cannot compensate for failure of a required condition.

The applicable OMNIBUS commitments include present consent, release from compelled labor and inherited or collective debt, care without custody, and behavioral correction and release. OMNIBUS v7.79-r1 §10 retains its **SUPPORTED RECONSTRUCTION** notice. These four files are component specifications; they do not claim to replace a complete governing OMNIBUS edition.

The Reflex resource margin uses one resource, unit, boundary and interval with disjoint task, coordination, maintenance and regeneration uses. A budget is not automatically a measured stock balance, and time does not accumulate as transferable stored credit. Named capacity gains/losses need their own observations. Spending on regeneration does not prove regeneration. Extraction, maintenance, surplus and regeneration may coexist across different resources. Return keeps chart, admissibility and context changes as a tuple, including MIXED outcomes.

Physical energy, entropy and exergy have separate units and assumptions. Phase Lock now carries the corrected energy balance and the reference-temperature conversion for exergy destruction instead of merely setting the earlier mixed-unit equation aside.

### Separate clocks, predictions, and coupling claims

Current output recovery, next-output recovery, autonomous retained-state evolution and residual closure have different witnesses. A deterministic residual counterexample establishes failure for its declared projection and update. Stochastic closure compares entire next-observation laws, not isolated realized values. A posterior can be sufficient without being minimal. Finite-history and changed-kernel claims retain their separate domains.

Request onset, readiness, release, first usable output and completion now have distinct event fields. Mean, SD, duration count, interruption frequency, person-hours and elapsed time retain their separate definitions. The exact cyclic timing example demonstrates lost order and a different next value; it is not a human-performance result.

Temporal v0.3's causal-padding frontier remains a conditional mathematical result for fixed readiness. The revisions adopt no padding policy. A timing comparison must distinguish the practical delay/regularity tradeoff from an interaction and from total task outcome. Physical tick gates and raw timestamps cannot be repaired by smoothing a display.

Phase Lock also adapts the WOBBLE coupling receipt: source, intermediate mechanism, target, eligible observations, lag/window, counter-signals and joined provenance. Co-movement alone does not supply that receipt. No WOBBLE economic evidence or phase classification is imported.
