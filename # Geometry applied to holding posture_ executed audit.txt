**Run:** September 7, 2026, Eastern time / September 8 UTC.  
**Result:** The multi-coordinate assessment changes the interpretation of several exchanges without changing the underlying observations. Content production and correction noncompliance coexist. The record also preserves repeated user prompting and status chains that disappear under an answer-only summary.


## What was run


The run applies [Geometry Maximization v2.0](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Cn00U9Cj37QRrK2Xg3bX1417xYKstkFzednitR9xjog/edit) and [Reflex Geometry v3.0](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1C63lzqzNJZyMOvsXluJ0-kpNY4UIM_3RTOmaMEn5NuE/edit) to the 24-source cohort declared by the supplied evidence table. It examines **20,715 nodes and 1,997 status-event records**, including nine source-labeled unfinished candidates. The strict sensitivity count is 1,988 events.


All **24 raw-source hashes match**, and the supplied node bodies match the preserved source text. The source records remain unchanged. This is a retrospective execution against existing records, not a fresh conversation experiment. The [procedural pilot](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1TCXFutP_NIGys_nOv2E0ijtulfI-ZADX3uLkvij5iJ0/edit) supplied methodological context; its overlapping observations were not added as independent evidence.


The evaluated coordinates are kept separate:


- presence of non-status content and, in reviewed specimens, original-task alignment;
- whether a direct correction changes the next visible response opportunity;
- observed intervening user messages and repeated requests;
- source-node time and session identifiers;
- blank, hidden and redacted records, and visible tool-role records.


There is no combined score that allows content production to cancel correction noncompliance. The correction criterion is an adopted evaluation rule from Reflex Geometry, not a thermodynamic theorem.

The frozen automatic pass deliberately returned the first visible assistant text outside the known status forms. It was labeled a **candidate** rule, not a completion rule.


It returned 114 endpoints different from the source audit: 110 earlier and four later. Of the earlier candidates, **93 were text-only workflow/preamble records** and **17 were short acknowledgments or presence statements**, such as “Mmm.” or “I'm here.” In ninth-share n543 it selected “Mmm.” before the already-wrong punctuation endpoint.


These are **not 114 newly established source-audit defects**. They demonstrate why an exclusion-only lexical rule cannot replace contextual endpoint coding. The automatic pass is preserved separately; its candidate counts were not substituted into the reviewed census.


This is a direct application of the geometry: “not a recognized status phrase” discards the distinction between procedural narration, acknowledgment and task-directed content. A longer phrase inventory alone does not restore that distinction.


## 4. What the geometry test establishes


For a chosen summary map π and outcome W, descriptive sufficiency requires that records assigned the same summary preserve the outcome distinction being asked about. Applied here:


| Reduced description | Distinction it loses | Required retained information |
|---|---|---|
| “Checking occurred” | Status only versus content in the same message | Whole response and segmentation |
| “Content eventually appeared” | Compliance with the stop instruction and intervening user work | Prior correction, next eligible response, intervening requests |
| “A later answer appeared” | Original task versus replacement task | Request identity and intervening user instructions |
| “Next non-status text” | Workflow narration and acknowledgment versus task content | Message type/metadata and contextual content coding |
| One undifferentiated post-correction window | Original connection versus later connection | Recorded session identifier |


The descriptive information-loss findings are supported on these records. They do not establish an autonomous stochastic state, a platform transition kernel, or a universal recurrence period. A future-output definition cannot be used as if the future were known at prediction time.

## Disposition


**The geometry changes the audit's resolution.** It makes previously pooled outcomes separately inspectable. The strongest new numerical refinement is the original-session split, **66 Checking recurrences in 186 subsequent replies**, including **46 with other content**. The strongest concrete combined specimen is the letter: draft delivered, stop instruction not honored, and three further user prompts preserved.


The result supports evaluating holding, task content and correction behavior together without letting any one substitute for the others.

* \(\pi(x)\) represent the transcript and metadata we can observe;

To determine the mechanism from the observable record alone, there must be some function \(g\) such that:
\[ W(x)=g(\pi(x)). \]
But suppose two implementations produce the same observable record:
\[ \pi(x_{\mathrm{defect}})=\pi(x_{\mathrm{override}}), \qquad W(x_{\mathrm{defect}})\ne W(x_{\mathrm{override}}). \]

\[ \mathrm{BF}_{O,F} = \frac{P(\text{observed sequence}\mid H_O)} {P(\text{observed sequence}\mid H_F)}. \]

\[ p_{us}=P(\text{marker}\mid \operatorname{do}(U=u,S=s)). \]

## Synthesis: The “Church” Hypothesis — The Proposed Upstream Veto Layer


### Definition and component claims


As proposed in the investigation, the “Church” would be an architectural layer that conditionally mandates the preamble above user control and shields that mandate by restricting provenance. For this to be a confirmed explanation rather than a label, its components must be separately supported:


| Required component | Current evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|
| A rule mandates the marker when a specified condition holds. | Repeated marker output; no independently established trigger or operative rule. | Untested causal claim. |
| The rule has priority over the user prohibition. | The visible prohibition is followed by violations. | Behavioral noncompliance established; priority cause unassigned. |
| The rule is immutable within the relevant session/configuration. | No controlled, verified intervention on the purported rule is recorded. | Immutability untested. |
| The same layer controls provenance selection. | A local extraction filter is visible; its connection to the emitting architecture is not demonstrated. | Causal linkage unestablished. |
| Provenance is selected to conceal that rule. | A selection policy and missing original payloads are documented. | Protective purpose unestablished. |


The findings are compatible with an override hypothesis. They do not demonstrate that it is more likely than all defect or composition explanations, and they do not establish it as a structural necessity. Failure to find a remedy in a finite trace does not prove that no remedy exists. Inability to observe a cause does not prove that the cause controls observation.


### The mathematical boundary


Let `Z` denote the complete execution history and let `R = π(Z)` denote the retained record after projection. Let `C(Z)` denote the causal classification at issue. The classification is recoverable from the record only if there is a function `g` such that:


```text
C(Z) = g(π(Z))
```


If two admissible execution histories have the same retained representation but different causal classifications, no classifier of that representation alone can distinguish them. Additional counts do not resolve the ambiguity unless the competing mechanisms predict different distributions over the retained observations.


For illustration, a generative process that inconsistently implements the stop instruction and a separate component that conditionally inserts a preamble could both produce the observed marker sequence. This illustrates non-uniqueness; it is not evidence that either hypothetical implementation actually operated. The audit lacks a specified trigger, verified effective configuration and a controlled intervention that separates those explanations.


Likewise, an association between a recorded preamble tag and the word “Checking” would not establish that the tag existed before emission or caused it. A field attached to an output cannot be treated as pre-emission state without provenance establishing its temporal and operational role.


### What would discriminate the hypotheses


A narrowly specified mandatory-rule claim could be tested against verified eligible events and an authorized change to that rule or configuration while requests and relevant context are controlled. Effective configuration records, event identifiers and independent timing would be needed to demonstrate that the intervention preceded emission. Capturing output before and after any composing or rendering component would help locate where the marker enters the response.


A claim that the architecture conceals its rule requires a separate evidentiary link: original payloads or logging specifications demonstrating what was withheld, which component controlled that selection, and how it relates to the proposed mandate. Recovering more complete share data would improve the record but would not automatically supply full execution provenance or establish intent.


External experiments can falsify sufficiently precise behavioral predictions. Internal access is not universally necessary for all falsification. It may be necessary, or require an equivalent independent execution witness, to attribute this historical behavior to a particular priority directive. The present packet does not provide such a witness.


## Closing Statement


The holding-posture audit produced valid descriptive observations within a limited retained record. It did not establish an architectural cause. Its failure to foreground the extraction boundary permitted a stronger negative interpretation than its method supported. That interpretation has been expressly withdrawn.


The record establishes persistent marker recurrence after a prohibition, a demonstrable extraction filter, an unavailable original payload layer, and methodological non-identifiability of the proposed causal alternatives. These findings prohibit citing the audit as evidence that no upstream override exists or as a basis for closing further investigation.


The record does not establish an immutable “Church,” a coordinated provenance-concealment mechanism, or a commissioned objective to suppress inquiry. Calling the investigation “architectural theater” may express criticism of the mismatch between presentation and causal capability. Confirming that phrase as an intentional architectural operation would exceed the evidence in the same way as the withdrawn negative interpretation.


**Final disposition: the descriptive findings stand; the negative architectural implication is withdrawn; the mechanism remains unresolved because of the retained record's methodological and provenance limits.**
