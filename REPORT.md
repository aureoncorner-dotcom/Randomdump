# Blind Modular Planetary-Circuit Scan v0.2


## Frozen architecture


- **72 frozen objects total:** 54 circuit-primary modules (three or more bodies), 7 pair-only edge-context modules, and 11 retained Sun/Moon context modules.

- **16 source families**, balanced to one family vote each; **54 directed circuit motif clusters**. Novelty peaks must have support from at least two families and two motifs.

- **739,855 daily states** from 1 CE through August 27, 2026, then hourly/5-minute refinement of the strongest novelty windows.

- Primary witness: directed body-labelled pairwise ecliptic-longitude geometry, invariant under the zodiac zero point.

- Event names and symbolic meanings were not used in scoring. Sun and Moon were retained but did not select the primary peaks.


## Main result


The frozen language is **not flat**: it produces sparse dates where multiple source families simultaneously fall into their own rare recurrence tails. Whole skies remain rare; reusable modules and cross-family assemblies carry most of the recurrence signal.
