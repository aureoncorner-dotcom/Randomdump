## 0. Controlling rules

1. **Primary instrument controls** the factual proposition it actually resolves.
2. Verified secondary reporting may support a proposition within its own scope but does not inherit primary-source weight.
3. Source-bearing reconciliation outranks source-free audit language.
4. Repeated summaries of the same source family are one evidentiary brick, not independent corroboration.
5. Model agreement has **zero external-world evidentiary weight**.
6. A route is not a join; a join is not a consequence; a consequence is not intent.
7. A later correction changes the current disposition but does not delete the earlier receipt.
8. `not found`, `not produced`, `no response`, `withheld`, `does not exist`, and `no individualized instrument required` are different states.
9. Symbolic material may nominate a question but may not upgrade a human-side claim.
10. Findings in the system/process lane do not identify a hidden human operator without an independent identity/causal bridge.

### Controlled fields

- **Finding type:** `FACT`, `ARCHITECTURE`, `LEGAL ELEMENT`, `CAUSAL JOIN`, `INTENT / CONSIDERATION`, `PROVENANCE / PROCESS`

- **Action:** `ADVANCE`, `HOLD`, `CLOSE ON CURRENT RECORD`, `WRONG INSTRUMENT`
